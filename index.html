DevTools → Application → IndexedDB. You'll see your encrypted records stored locally. Open one — it's ciphertext, unreadable without your PIN. Your data lives on your machine, and only on your machine.

Frequently asked

Quick answers.

How much can I store now?
WaceSpork now uses IndexedDB, which gives you dramatically more room than traditional browser storage. Modern browsers typically allocate hundreds of megabytes to several gigabytes per origin — enough for thousands of documents, notes, contacts, and a full Personal Album. Exact limits vary by browser and device, but you can archive far more than ever before, all encrypted and fully local.
Why IndexedDB instead of localStorage?
localStorage caps out around 5 MB and only stores strings — far too limiting for a real workspace. IndexedDB handles large binary data efficiently, supports transactions, and scales to browser-defined limits that are hundreds of times larger. Your encrypted workspace stays on your device, but now with the capacity to actually archive files safely.
How do I know you're not collecting my data?
Because there is nowhere for data to go. WaceSpork has no server, no backend, no API, and no analytics. It's a single HTML file running entirely in your browser. You can prove it yourself: open DevTools → Network tab, use every feature, and watch for outbound requests. You'll see none. You can even disconnect from the internet completely and keep working.
Do I need to install anything?
No. It's a single HTML file that runs in your browser. Double-click and go.
Does it work offline?
Yes, completely. Once you have the file, you never need the internet again unless you click a shortcut that opens an external website. Every module — Notepad, Contacts, Meetings, Personal Album, Personal Drive — works with zero connection.
Is there a subscription?
No. $19 one-time. No recurring charges, no login server, no upsells. Free updates for life.
Is my IndexedDB data still portable?
Yes. Export an encrypted backup from IndexedDB and import it on any device. Your entire archive — notes, contacts, meetings, album images, drive files — travels as a single encrypted file. No cloud, no account, no lock-in.
Does IndexedDB change how backups work?
The backup format stays the same — one encrypted file you can save anywhere. The difference is capacity: you can now back up and restore much larger workspaces than the old 5 MB ceiling allowed. Export regularly and keep your PIN safe.
What happens if I forget my PIN?
You can reset the workspace and start fresh, but your encrypted data becomes unrecoverable. There's no recovery code and no backdoor — that's what keeps your data safe from everyone, including us. Export regular encrypted backups and remember your PIN.
Which browsers work?
Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox, and any modern Chromium-based browser. IndexedDB is supported in all of them.
Does the demo expire?
No. The demo works forever with the same 3-item limits. Upgrade whenever you're ready.
Can I use it on multiple devices?
Yes. Export an encrypted backup from one device, import it on another. Your entire workspace travels with the file — no sync server required.
